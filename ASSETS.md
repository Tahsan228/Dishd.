# Visual assets

All people, kitchens, pickup windows, ratings, and diary entries in this milestone are fictional demo content. Photographs illustrate dishes; they are not evidence about a real cook or halal sourcing.

## Original hero photograph

Saved asset: `public/images/biryani.png`.

Tool: built-in image_gen, generation mode (new image, no input/reference image). Generated on 2026-09-05, 1536 × 1024. The original output was copied into the project without editing.

Prompt submitted:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial food photograph for Dishd, a premium neighborhood halal home-cooking discovery website. Generate one wide landscape photo, approximately 1536 by 1024. An abundant, freshly home-cooked chicken biryani in a large shallow off-white ceramic serving bowl on a warm ivory linen table, fragrant separate long-grain rice, saffron colors, tender roasted chicken, fried onions, bright cilantro and mint, a little bowl of cucumber raita and cut lemon off to one side, beautifully imperfect domestic table setting. Close overhead three-quarter camera angle, bowl fills most of frame, natural afternoon window light from the left, soft realistic shadows, authentic editorial cookbook photography, tactile food detail, warm cream and subtle deep forest-green fabric accents, deeply appetizing and believable. No text, no logos, no lettering, no border, no collage, no graphic decorations, no alcohol, no people. The whole photograph is food/table, no empty area reserved for text.
